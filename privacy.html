Who We Are and What This Policy Covers

LearnTogether ("we", "us", "our") operates the LearnTogether learning portal — a curated educational platform that connects learners with quality learning resources.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the platform.

03 — Usage

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate the LearnTogether platform. Specifically we use it to:

  • Authenticate you and maintain your login session securely
  • Display your course progress and completion status
  • Show your bookmarked courses across sessions
  • Allow the administrator to monitor platform engagement and provide support
  • Show your reviews and ratings to other users of the platform
  • Track whether you are currently active on the platform
  • Send email verification when you create an admin account

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling to third parties, or any purpose beyond operating this educational platform.

04 — Storage

Where Your Data is Stored

All data is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore database and Firebase Authentication), which is Google's secure cloud infrastructure.

  • Firebase Firestore — stores your username, progress, bookmarks, activity logs and reviews
  • Firebase Authentication — stores admin email and securely manages authentication tokens
  • Your browser — stores your theme preference (dark/light mode) only in localStorage

Google Firebase complies with GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001, and other major data protection standards. Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

Data retention: Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If your account is deleted by the administrator, all associated data is permanently removed from Firestore.
05 — Sharing

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Your data may only be accessed in the following limited circumstances:

  • Platform Administrator — the admin of your LearnTogether portal can see your username, progress percentage, online status, and activity log as part of platform management
  • Google Firebase — as our infrastructure provider, Google processes data per their own privacy policy and data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements — if required by law, court order, or government authority, we may be required to disclose information
  • Other Users — your username and star ratings/reviews are visible to other users of the same platform
06 — Security

How We Protect Your Data

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords are stored as hashed values — never as plain text
  • Admin accounts require email verification before access is granted
  • Admin logout requires OTP (one-time password) verification for an additional layer of security
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict database access to authorised requests only
  • Firebase Authentication handles all admin credentials using Google's secure infrastructure

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

07 — Your Rights

Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access — you can request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to Correction — you can update your password at any time through the platform settings
  • Right to Deletion — you can request that your account and all associated data be deleted by contacting the platform administrator
  • Right to Portability — you can request an export of your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object — you can object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in the Contact section below or reach out to your platform administrator directly.

08 — Cookies

Cookies and Local Storage

LearnTogether uses minimal browser storage:

  • localStorage (kp-theme) — stores your dark/light theme preference. This is stored only on your device and never transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser storage.
  • Firebase Authentication tokens — Firebase stores authentication session tokens in your browser to keep you logged in. These are managed by Google Firebase and expire automatically.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics cookies. We do not track you across other websites.

09 — Children

Children's Privacy

LearnTogether is designed as an educational platform and may be used by learners of all ages. However, user accounts are created exclusively by the platform administrator — users cannot self-register.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has been provided an account without your knowledge, please contact the platform administrator immediately.

Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
